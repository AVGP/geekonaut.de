Hi! I'm **Martin Splitt** — known across the internet as **@splitti.online**.

I've been on this blue planet since **1989**. I started out in northern Germany, moved to eastern Germany to study Computer Science at **TU Ilmenau**, and eventually settled in **Zurich, Switzerland**.

### What I Do in Cyberspace

I am a software developer and open-source contributor with two decades of experience across web architecture, Linux and Windows kernel space, 3D graphics in the browser, and embedded systems.

Since 2018, I've been part of the **Google Search Relations** team, helping developers and SEO professionals understand how search engines crawl, render, and index the modern web — and co-hosting Google's **Search Off the Record** podcast.

Before that, I served as Head of Engineering and *Bit Magician* at **Archilogic**, building interactive 3D architectural models in the browser with WebGL and Three.js.

### What I Do Away From Keyboard (AFK)

* **Underwater:** I enjoy and teach scuba diving at [divingfor.fun](https://divingfor.fun) — yes, even when Swiss lakes drop to 4°C in winter.
* **In the Darkroom:** I take portraits at [throughthelens.ch](https://throughthelens.ch), run a Swiss zine project, and create tactile photo art using 19th-century chemical processes (cyanotypes, salt prints, and wet-plate experiments) at [arsphotographica.ch](https://arsphotographica.ch).
* **Writing with Friends:** I co-write [thatsaweb.site](https://thatsaweb.site) with Eva, celebrating weird, human, independent websites.
